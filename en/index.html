Split the honey.
Just right.

Each time honey lands on a hex cell, it splits in half down-left and down-right. Place lids to change its path and fill every pot with exactly the right amount.

HOW TO PLAY

Place lids. Pour. That's it.

蓋

Lids change the path

Tap a cell to place a lid, tap again to remove it. You only get so many.

流

Pour and see

Honey flows down the comb into the pots. Get every pot right and the bees cheer.

割

Break cracked walls

Tap a cracked wall and a bee chews it away. Some stages pour from two spouts.

日

Daily puzzle and hints

A new puzzle every day. Stuck? Watch a short video to light up the next move.
