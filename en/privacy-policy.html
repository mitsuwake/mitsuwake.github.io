Privacy Policy

iPhone app "みつわけハニカム" (Mitsuwake Honeycomb)

This policy explains how the developer of the iPhone app "みつわけハニカム" (the "App") handles information about its users.

1. Information the App itself collects

The App has no accounts and no sign-in. The developer does not collect your name, email address, phone number or any other personal information.

Your stage progress, Daily puzzle record, read notices and settings (sound, vibration) are stored only on your device (UserDefaults) and are never sent to the developer. Deleting the App deletes them.

To show notices, the App downloads notices.json from this website. No user information is sent with that request (as with any web page, GitHub Pages, which hosts this site, may keep ordinary access logs).

2. Information sent for advertising

To keep the App free, it shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK included in the App may send the following to Google:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising identifier (IDFA) — the IDFA only if you allow tracking (see section 4)
  • Approximate location estimated from your IP address
  • Information about ads shown and your interactions with them (taps, video playback)
  • Information about app launches and usage
  • Device model, OS version, and app performance and crash information

The developer does not receive this information and does not use it to identify you. The developer only receives aggregated numbers from Google, such as ad impressions and revenue.

3. How the information is used

  • To show ads, including ads based on your interests
  • To measure ad performance and prevent ad fraud
  • To find and fix problems in the ad service

See Google's pages for how Google handles this information:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google advertising: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. Consent and tracking permission

The first time you open a stage (the game board), the App shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) where required, followed by Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asking whether you allow tracking with the advertising identifier (IDFA). If you do not allow it, every feature of the App still works the same; ads will simply not be personalized.

You can change your choice at any time in the iPhone Settings app → Privacy & Security → Tracking → みつわけハニカム.

You can also manage ad personalization in Google's My Ad Center: https://myadcenter.google.com/

5. In-app purchase

The App has no in-app purchases and handles no payment information.

6. Children

The App is suitable for a wide audience but is not designed specifically for children. The developer does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Parents can turn off tracking as described in section 4.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated when the App or the law changes. Updates are posted on this page with a new date.
